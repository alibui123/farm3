# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion + GSAP/ScrollTrigger + Lenis + lucide-react — specified in the build brief.

## Users

Primary: Pakistani households seeking trustworthy, farm-fresh cow and buffalo milk delivered to the door each morning (B2C). Secondary: milk distributors / gawalay who need reliable bulk supply (B2B).

## Product Purpose

Al-shukr Dairy Farm sells and delivers hygienic, unadulterated fresh milk from its own farm. The homepage's job is to earn trust and convert visitors into free-sample requests and WhatsApp conversations.

## Positioning

Farm-owned milk with morning delivery and a chilled chain — not adulterated bazaar milk. Traceability and lab testing are part of the promise. Placeholder proof figures live in `/lib/constants.ts` and must be labeled as illustrative until replaced with real numbers.

## Brand Commitments

- Legal / display name: **Al-shukr Dairy Farm** (never Finova).
- Short mark: **Al-shukr**.
- Market: Pakistan; Urdu-friendly future copy is open, English homepage for now.
- Contact CTA: WhatsApp (`wa.me`) with pre-filled interest message; phone/address placeholders until real details are supplied.
- Visual constraint (brief-pinned): "Golden hour on the farm" — cream base, pasture green primary, sunrise amber accent, soft glass, organic milk-wave dividers. Palette and type faces specified in the build brief override category defaults.

## Accessibility

Keyboard-accessible navigation and form; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; semantic HTML; meaningful alt text.

## Open Decisions

- Real WhatsApp number, phone, address, farm photos, hero video, and verified stats (households, years, animals) — placeholders marked in constants until provided.
- Backend (Supabase sample insert) deferred; form handler left as TODO.
